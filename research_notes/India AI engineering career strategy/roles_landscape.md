# India Engineering Roles Landscape (Nov 2026) for a ~2-YOE AI Engineer / FDE Moving from Tokyo

Research date: 2026-11-27. Every data point carries the date the source gives. "Indeed MCP" means a live Indeed India job search run on 2026-11-27. Those results are individual listings, not totals. Glassdoor counts come from cached search-page titles, and their snapshot month is shown. They change often and include near-duplicates, so treat them as rough sizes, not exact counts.

## Q1. Demand, companies, skills, experience, difficulty, outlook and progression by role family

### Takeaway
AI/ML is the fastest-growing slice of Indian white-collar hiring in 2026. Naukri AI/ML postings rose 20 to 49% year on year (YoY) across 2026 releases, while general IT/software was flat to slightly negative. LinkedIn ranks AI Engineer #2 nationally and #1 in Bengaluru and Hyderabad for 2026. Classic SDE and backend roles are still the largest pool in absolute numbers but are growing slowly. The FDE title went from almost nothing to an established category in India in 2026, starting from a small base.

### Inferences
- **Demand ranking for a 2-YOE candidate in India, Nov 2026 (estimate):**
  - AI Engineer / GenAI / Agentic AI Engineer: tens of thousands of listings, growing about 20–35% YoY. This is the largest growing pool.
  - SDE/Backend (AI-augmented): very large but flat. This is the safest fallback.
  - ML Engineer: large, but many JDs want classical ML or model training.
  - FDE: hundreds of listings, growing very fast from a tiny base.
  - Emerging titles: tens, often senior.
- **Interview difficulty (inference from JD patterns and the sources above; there is no direct difficulty survey):**
  - FAANG/product SDE-2: DSA plus LLD/HLD system design. The highest bar for raw coding.
  - AI Engineer at product companies and GCCs: typically coding, LLM system design (RAG/agents/evals) and project deep-dives.
  - FDE: coding, customer or case-style problem decomposition, and communication rounds.
  - Applied AI Architect at frontier labs: 10+ years required (see Q2). Not reachable at 2 YOE.
- **Progression paths (inference; only the Pragmatic Engineer role definition is sourced):**
  - FDE → Senior/Staff FDE (Databricks has Staff FDE and manager roles in India) → Solutions Architect, Applied AI Architect, engineering/FDE manager, or founder.
  - AI Engineer → Senior → Staff/Principal AI Engineer or AI Platform lead.
  - SDE → SDE-3 → Staff/Principal.
- **2-year outlook (Nov 2026 to Nov 2028), inference:**
  - AI Engineer and agentic titles will probably keep absorbing SDE demand, because GCCs (+18%) and Indian MNCs (+82% AI/ML in Feb) are expanding.
  - FDE growth is likely to slow from 19x but stay high, since Indeed calls it "a structural shift."
  - Evals and agent reliability work will probably be folded into AI Engineer and Agent Engineer JDs rather than becoming large standalone title pools in India.

## Q2. Which companies use the "Forward Deployed Engineer" title in India? Is India FDE hiring real and growing?

### Takeaway
Yes. India FDE hiring is real, on-site and growing, and as of Oct–Nov 2026 it is no longer mostly US or remote. Big Tech has opened India-based FDE roles: Google Cloud, YouTube, Meta, Apple, Cisco and Databricks, among others. AI-native startups and Indian SaaS companies hire the title too. Frontier labs are the exception: OpenAI, Anthropic and Palantir list FDEs, but none in India. Their India customer-facing roles are "Applied AI Architect" and "Applied AI Engineer," which are senior.

### Inferences
- **India FDE hiring has three tiers (inference):**
  1. **Big Tech GenAI FDEs** (Google Cloud, YouTube, Meta, Apple, Cisco). These use internal leveling; "FDE V" suggests senior, while non-prefixed titles may fit around 2–5 years. This tier probably pays best.

## Q3. How do AI Engineer, ML Engineer and SDE-with-AI roles differ in Indian JDs and pay?

### Takeaway
In Indian 2026 JDs:
- **AI Engineer / GenAI / Agentic** means building LLM applications: RAG, agents, evals, Azure/AWS/GCP AI services.
- **ML Engineer** leans toward model training, classical ML, data pipelines and MLOps.
- **SDE-with-AI** is a standard SDE ladder role on LLM-adjacent systems, such as inference capacity or AI-powered SRE.

Aggregators show ML Engineer pay slightly above AI Engineer at the same experience, with GenAI/LLM specialists at a premium. SDE-2 at top product companies and FAANG pays more than either generic AI or ML band.

- The "AI Engineer" title spans a very wide pay range in India. Services firms and GCC entry roles sit near ₹8–15 LPA. AI-first product companies and Big Tech, where it is often levelled as SDE-2 or MLE-2, can match or beat SDE-2 bands.

## Q4. Which roles best fit a 2-YOE candidate (SDE-2 / Engineer II), and how do roles rank on attractiveness and transition likelihood for this profile?

### Takeaway
The best fits are FDE / AI FDE and AI Engineer (GenAI/Agentic/Applied AI) at product companies, GCCs, Big Tech GenAI teams and AI-native startups. Both map directly onto the candidate's experience, and a growing set of India JDs accept 1–3 years. Frontier-lab Applied AI Architect, senior FDE (5–6+ years) and specialist inference or ML-research roles are stretch or out-of-reach targets until about 2028.
