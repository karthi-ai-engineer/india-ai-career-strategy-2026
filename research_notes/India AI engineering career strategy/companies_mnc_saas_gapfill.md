Research date: 2026-11-27. About 30 tool calls (WebSearch + WebFetch). Labels: **[V]** = verified against the cited page or a primary/major-press source in this session. **[S]** = taken from search-result snippets or aggregators (Built In, AnitaB, Bayt, UnifyGTM, Revelio) and not opened in full. **[I]** = my inference. Careers URLs marked "(seen)" turned up in results. Those marked "(not verified)" are the usual pattern for that company and were not opened.

Comp caveat for every row: the Levels.fyi pages fetched on 2026-11-27 all showed "last updated November 27, 2026". That looks like the page render date, not the date of the data. They show **median total comp per level only**: no base/stock split, no sample counts, no percentiles. A ~2–4 YOE engineer usually maps to the 2nd level (IC2 / SE2 / T2–T3) **[I]**.

## Q1. Priority Category A companies (Adobe, Uber, LinkedIn, Oracle OCI, Intuit, Walmart): India presence, roles, stack, comp, expansion

### Takeaway
All six have large, long-standing India engineering centres, and in 2025–2026 they posted GenAI/agentic roles in India. Uber (May 2026: about 9,600-seat campuses due by end-2027) and LinkedIn (Bengaluru is its largest R&D hub outside Silicon Valley) show the clearest growth signals. Walmart's CTO said in January 2026 that India headcount will stay roughly flat while roles change. Among the companies fetched here, LinkedIn pays the most in India: IC2 median about ₹62.6 LPA. Oracle (IC-2 about ₹27.6 LPA) pays the least.

### Inferences
- **[I]** For an AI Eng/FDE profile, Adobe has the most explicit India "FDE" titles among Category A (Firefly FDE manager, FDE Full-Stack AI). Intuit (GenOS core team in India) and LinkedIn (applied ML in Bengaluru) are the strongest for "AI platform engineer" work.
- **[I]** At ~2 YOE, LinkedIn IC2 (about ₹63 L) and Intuit SE2 (about ₹47 L) are the top India comp bands found. Oracle IC-2 (about ₹28 L) is a "stepping-stone" band.
- **[I]** Uber's campuses due by end-2027 line up well with joining in 2027–2028. Seat capacity is not a hiring commitment, though.
- **[I]** Walmart's flat-headcount statement means fewer net-new seats. Hiring will be mix-shift toward AI roles.

### Gaps
- India headcount for Oracle, Intuit and Uber in 2026 has no official figure. Uber's ">750 engineers" is old.
- Primary engineering-blog stack evidence (Uber, LinkedIn, Walmart, Adobe tech blogs) was not fetched within the budget. Stack claims above are limited to GenOS, AWS CDK and job titles.
- The date of Uber's "close to 250 engineers" announcement could not be confirmed.
