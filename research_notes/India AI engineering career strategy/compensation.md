# India Software / AI Engineering Compensation Benchmark (1–4 YOE, focus ~2 YOE), as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-27. All Levels.fyi figures below were fetched on 2026-11-26/27; the pages showed "Last Updated: November 26/27, 2026". They are **reported (self-reported, partly verified) median total compensation (TC)** in INR. TC on Levels.fyi = base + annualised stock + bonus. 1 crore = 100 LPA.

**General limitations (apply throughout):**
- Levels.fyi company pages give per-level *medians* only. They give no per-level sample sizes and no base/stock/bonus split; the site says that data is only available through its MCP server, which was not used. Medians from small samples (for example Palantir, CRED, Rubrik, Stripe India) can move a lot when a few entries change.
- Levels.fyi data comes mostly from product companies and leans toward higher earners. It skews well above AmbitionBox, Glassdoor and Indeed. For example, Indeed's average base for software engineers in India is ₹8.86 LPA from 16.8k reports ([Indeed India](https://in.indeed.com/career/software-engineer/salaries)).
- RSU-heavy TC moves with exchange rates and share prices. Year-1 TC can include sign-on bonuses.
- Matching a level to "~2 YOE" is **my inference** based on common ladder conventions, not something the source states. It is marked "(inferred mapping)".

## 1. Levels.fyi India: median TC by company and level (reported data)

### Takeaway
At about 2 YOE, the "SDE-2-equivalent" level ranges from about ₹34–46 LPA TC at Indian unicorns, bank GCCs and mid-tier MNC levels (Flipkart SDE2, Swiggy L7, Microsoft 61, Goldman Associate, PhonePe SE2) to about ₹63–70 LPA at Big Tech L4/SDE II (Google L4, Amazon SDE II, Uber L4, CRED L4). It reaches about ₹1 Cr at top-tier infra and fintech firms (Databricks L4, Rubrik L4, Stripe L2). The overall India software engineer median is ₹30.8 LPA.

## 2. AI / ML / GenAI premium over standard SDE roles in India

### Takeaway
Reported data is mixed. Levels.fyi's self-reported "ML/AI software engineer" median for India (₹29.0L, n = 1,297) is actually *slightly below* the overall software engineer median (₹30.8L). The premium shows up in **specialised GenAI/LLM, MLOps and AI-infra roles, and in hiring at GCCs and AI-first companies**. In report and recruiter data it is about 20–30% at 0–2 YOE, widening to 25–60% at mid/senior levels. It goes mainly to engineers with production LLM experience.

### Cited Findings
- Levels.fyi ML/AI software engineers in India: median TC ₹28,96,764 (P25 ₹14.5L, P75 ₹50.8L, P90 ₹73.7L), n = 1,297. Updated 2026-11-26. — [Levels.fyi ML/AI India](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/focus/ml-ai/locations/india). For comparison, all software engineers: median ₹30.8L, n = 20,834. — [Levels.fyi India SWE](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/india)
- **TeamLease Digital (GCC analysis of 37,000 tech and digital roles, Oct 2026):** median *fixed* pay at 0–2 YOE is ₹13.2 LPA for a GenAI Engineer, ₹10.8 LPA for a Data Scientist, ₹10.2 LPA for a Data Engineer and ₹9.0 LPA for a Cybersecurity Engineer. At 15+ YOE, an AI Architect earns ₹108.6 LPA. AI Architect, Lead GenAI Engineer and Cloud Security Architect roles reach ₹97–109 LPA. — [CXOToday / TeamLease Digital](https://cxotoday.com/others/teamlease-digital-44-of-indian-gccs-own-full-platforms-ai-outcomes-as-compensation-tops-₹108-6-lpa/)
- **TeamLease Digital, Digital Skills & Salary Primer FY2025-26 (Aug 2025):** there is one qualified engineer for every 10 open GenAI roles, and a 53% talent gap is projected by 2026. GenAI engineers and MLOps professionals are seeing annual increases above 18%. Senior GenAI salaries are projected to rise from ₹28 LPA (FY25) to ₹33.5 LPA (FY27). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/ai-boom-1-qualified-engineer-for-every-10-open-genai-roles-says-teamlease-report-3701061); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/indias-ai-market-grows-but-talent-gap-expected-to-reach/496465)

Michael Page's role-level AI salary bands were not found.

### Inferences
- From TeamLease's early-career fixed pay, a GenAI Engineer earns about 22% more than a Data Scientist and about 29% more than a Data Engineer. This is the most defensible "reported" early-career premium figure (fixed pay only, GCC sample).
- The Levels.fyi ML/AI median falling below the software engineer median likely reflects a mix effect. The ML/AI bucket includes many services and analytics firms and data-science-like roles, so being an "ML engineer" does not guarantee a premium. A premium depends on employer tier plus GenAI/LLM production or infrastructure skills.

### Gaps
- No primary-source figures were found from NASSCOM, Xpheno, Randstad, Quess or the AIM Research AI salary report. These need direct checks.
- No Levels.fyi split for "MLOps/AI infra" vs software engineer in India.
- The TeamLease figures are fixed pay at GCCs, not TC.

## 3. FDE / Solutions / Customer Engineer comp in India

### Takeaway
Hard data is thin. Levels.fyi has **no Palantir India data**. Solutions Architect pay in India has a median TC of about ₹45.9L across mostly mid/senior people. FDE figures come from blogs and job postings: about ₹18–28 LPA at 0–2 YOE and ₹28–55 LPA at 3–6 YOE, with founding-FDE roles at AI startups up to about ₹70–90 LPA.

### Cited Findings
- **Levels.fyi Solution Architect, India:** median TC ₹45.9L (P25 ₹34.5L, P75 ₹62.8L, P90 ₹81.1L), n = 334. Highest-paying companies: Cisco ₹78.3L, Amazon (AWS) ₹74.0L, JPMorgan ₹61.2L, Google ₹56.8L, SAP ₹55.4L. - **FDE India (blogs and postings, low reliability):** about ₹18–28 LPA at 0–2 YOE, ₹28–55 LPA at 3–6 YOE and ₹55–90+ LPA for seniors. Glassdoor's Bengaluru FDE average is about ₹17 LPA (range ₹11–36L), with only about 9 data points. Posting examples: Weekday 5+ YOE ₹25–40 LPA, Keyrus ₹22–34 LPA, Cartesia founding FDE ₹70–90 LPA. — [Taggd](https://taggd.in/blogs/forward-deployed-engineers/); [360DigitalTransformation](https://www.360digitaltransformation.com/blogs/guides/forward-deployed-engineer-salary-india); [fdeindia.live](https://fdeindia.live/fde-jobs/bengaluru/)

### Inferences
- At 2 YOE, an FDE or solutions role at a Big Tech or AI-infra MNC probably pays in line with that company's SDE ladder or slightly below it. Pre-sales roles also carry a variable component. Startup FDE roles are where any FDE premium sits, and those figures are weakly sourced.

### Gaps

## 4. Job-switch hike at ~2 YOE; value of Japan experience / relocation

### Takeaway
Average in-place increments are about 9% (Aon 2026). Surveyed expectations for a job switch are mostly 21–40%. Michael Page reports **up to 30%** for switchers in AI-type fields. In practice, at 2 YOE the biggest gain comes from a *level jump* (SDE I→SDE II at a higher-tier company), where Levels.fyi medians show about 2x. **No source** quantified how Indian employers value Japan experience.

### Cited Findings
- **Aon:** actual increments were 8.9% in 2025 and 9.0% in 2026, with 9.1% projected for 2027. Attrition rose to 18% in 2026 (16.2% in 2025). — [Aon 2027 release](https://www.aon.com/apac/in-the-press/asia-newsroom/2026/aon-study-finds-salaries-in-india-projected-to-fise-9-1-in-2027-employers-balance-growth-and-cost-pressures); [Aon 2026 release](https://www.aon.com/apac/in-the-press/asia-newsroom/2026/aon-survey-projects-slight-uptick-in-salaries-in-india-2026)
- **foundit Appraisal Trends 2026:** about 27% of respondents expect a 21–30% hike to switch jobs and 23% expect 31–40%. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/almost-everyone-is-looking-to-switch-jobs-but-only-if-employers-meet-this-pay-demand-544684-2026-07-23); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/personal-finance/70-per-cent-employees-expect-21-per-cent-plus-hike-to-switch-jobs-report)
- **Michael Page 2026:** switchers in AI, semiconductor and EV fields get up to a 30% hike. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/ai-ev-chip-roles-command-30-pay-hikes-three-times-market-average)
- **Blind (Oct 2026):** 35% of India-based tech professionals got no hike (30%) or a pay cut (5%). Seen in a search snippet; the page returned 402. — [Blind blog](https://www.teamblind.com/blog/1-in-3-indian-tech-workers-got-no-hike-or-a-pay-cut-this-year/)

### Inferences
- In India, offers are usually anchored to current CTC. Coming from Japan, an engineer may face either anchoring to a converted JPY salary or a reset to Indian bands. At ~2 YOE, Indian product-company bands at SDE II level (₹35–70 LPA) likely exceed a typical Japanese 2 YOE salary converted at nominal FX. This is an inference only; no JPY figures were researched here.
- Foreign or Japanese-company experience is probably valued most at Japan-linked GCCs or for Japan-market client-facing roles (FDE, solutions). This is not verified by any source.

### Gaps
- No data on relocation packages for India-bound hires or on how Japan experience affects offers. No LeetCode or Blind India 2 YOE offer threads were retrieved.

## 5. City differences and the highest-paying role + tech combinations

### Takeaway
On Levels.fyi, Bengaluru leads (median ₹37.1L), followed by Hyderabad (₹31.6L). Pune (₹21.1L) and Chennai (₹18.1L) are well below. Delhi NCR and Mumbai sit around ₹20–21L (2025 heatmap). Much of the gap reflects *employer mix* (top payers concentrate in Bengaluru and Hyderabad), not same-company pay differences. The highest early-career TC is at infra/data/fintech MNCs (Databricks, Rubrik, Stripe, Rippling, Atlassian, Uber) at the L4-equivalent level.

### Cited Findings
- **Levels.fyi (viewed 2026-11-26/27):**
  - Greater Bengaluru: median ₹37.1L (P75 ₹57.7L, P90 ₹79.6L), n = 11,405. Highest payers: Broadcom ₹1.32 Cr, Airbnb ₹1.19 Cr, Rippling ₹1.08 Cr, Rubrik ₹1.06 Cr, Stripe ₹1.02 Cr. — [Levels.fyi Bengaluru](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/greater-bengaluru)
  - Hyderabad: median ₹31.6L (P75 ₹50.2L, P90 ₹70.1L), n = 2,985. — [Levels.fyi Hyderabad](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/hyderabad-ind)
  - Pune: median ₹21.1L (P75 ₹32.8L, P90 ₹47.8L), n = 1,748. — [Levels.fyi Pune](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/pune-ind)
  - Chennai: median ₹18.1L (P75 ₹29.6L, P90 ₹46.4L), n = 995. — [Levels.fyi Chennai](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/chennai-ind)
