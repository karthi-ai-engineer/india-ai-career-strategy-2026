# Indian Tech Hiring Market Outlook 2026–2028 (AI Engineering, FDE, SWE)

Notes current as of 27 Nov 2026. Convention: **[FACT]** = reported data with a date; **[PROJECTION]** = a forecast made by a named source; **[SPECULATION]** = my own inference, not sourced. Most search results were secondary press; primary PDFs (NASSCOM, Zinnov, Naukri) were often not directly fetchable (Naukri blog returned HTTP 403), so the figures below come from press coverage of those primaries unless stated otherwise.

## Q1. Current state of Indian tech hiring (2025 – Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
Overall white-collar and IT hiring in India is flat to slightly positive. AI/ML hiring is growing about 20–30% year on year and is concentrated in experienced talent. IT services have resumed modest net hiring (Q2 FY27) after the 2025 TCS cuts, but fresher and generic software-development demand is weak. The market is splitting: AI, GCC and Big Tech roles are growing, while traditional IT services and junior coding roles are stagnant or shrinking.

## Q2. GCCs: count, projections, AI CoEs, hiring

### Takeaway
India has about 2,100 GCCs (Zinnov-NASSCOM 2026) with a market of about $98B, up from ~1,700 (2024) and ~1,580 in earlier counts. GCCs are the main destination for AI-related hiring in India, though overall GCC hiring is "moderate" and no Indian GCC is yet rated "AI-native".

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] Zinnov-NASSCOM India GCC Landscape Report 2026 ("GCC Value Orbit", press release 2–3 Jul 2026): 2,117 GCCs and a USD 98.4B market. India ranked #1 globally for AI hiring intensity among GCC markets and #2 to the US in enterprise AI talent — [Zinnov](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/); [PRNewswire, Jul 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/india-s-gccs-are-increasingly-leading-the-ai-mandate-for-global-enterprises-driving-global-value-creation-nasscom--zinnov-report-302817463.html)
- [FACT] Demand for AI-centric skills in GCCs rose 1.5 percentage points over the previous six months, while overall GCC hiring is expected to stay moderate — [PRNewswire, Jul 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/india-s-gccs-are-increasingly-leading-the-ai-mandate-for-global-enterprises-driving-global-value-creation-nasscom--zinnov-report-302817463.html)
- [FACT, secondary] More than 1,200 Indian GCCs have AI/ML capabilities, with over 250,000 AI professionals (~28% of the global GCC AI talent pool) — [DevX analysis](https://www.devx.work/blog/nasscom-zinnov-gcc-landscape-2026-analysis)
- [FACT, secondary] 3,728 units, ~2.36M employees, and a count up 32% since FY2021. Claims that "64% of new GCC roles require AI skills" and of "510,452 jobs in 2026" appear only in this recruiter guide and are unverified — [Alliance International](https://www.allianceinternational.co.in/how-to-hire-for-gcc-in-india-2026/)
- [FACT] The Zinnov-NASSCOM 2026 report finds 0% of Indian GCCs are "AI-native" yet — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/gcc/worlds-largest-ai-hiring-hub-yet-no-gcc-in-india-is-actually-ai-native)
- [FACT] India GCCs face a ~40% AI skills gap and are increasingly building talent internally instead of hiring it — [Business Today, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/jobs/story/indias-gccs-face-a-40-ai-skills-gap-why-theyre-building-talent-from-within-instead-of-just-hiring-543090-2026-07-15)
- [FACT] India holds about half of the world's GCC workforce, roughly 2M people (Feb 2026) — [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/h1b-visa-impact-india-tech-hiring-faamng/)
- [FACT] An earlier count of 1,580+ GCCs with a market over $46B is cited in H-1B commentary (older base) — [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/h1b-fee-impact-indian-it-cmua0rizh000f2znt4pgnozcc)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] GCCs are the most durable demand source for senior AI engineers and platform engineers in India through 2028. Counts grew from ~1,700 to ~2,100 in about two years, and multinationals have reasons to move capability onshore-to-India (H-1B costs, cost arbitrage). The 40% skills gap and the "0% AI-native" finding point to demand for people who can lead AI transformation, not just headcount.
- [SPECULATION] Earlier NASSCOM-Zinnov projections of ~2,400+ GCCs and $99–105B by 2030 were in training-era coverage. The 2026 report's $98.4B already approaches that range, which suggests the earlier projections were conservative. This is unverified; the 2030 projection in the 2026 report was not retrieved.

### Gaps
- The share of GCCs with formal AI CoEs and GCC hiring volumes for 2026 were not retrieved from a primary source. The 2030 projection in the 2026 report was not confirmed.

## Q3. Enterprise AI adoption and the AI talent gap

### Takeaway
Agentic AI is moving from experiments to scaled use in large enterprises: 40% of large organisations report scaling agents (McKinsey 2026), up from 27%. India's AI talent pool is projected to reach about 1.25M by 2027, while demand estimates run to 2.3M openings. That gap is the main structural support for AI engineering salaries.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] McKinsey State of AI 2025 (survey published ~Nov 2025): 23% of respondents' organisations were scaling an agentic AI system somewhere, 62% were at least experimenting with agents, and no more than 10% were scaling agents in any single function — [McKinsey 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2025)
- [FACT] McKinsey State of AI 2026 ("On the road to ROI"): 40% of large organisations (>$1B revenue) report scaling AI agents, up from 27% the year before. Smaller organisations are flat at 22% — [McKinsey 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai). Note that the 2025 and 2026 figures use different definitions and are not directly comparable.
- [PROJECTION] Deloitte-NASSCOM "Advancing India's AI Skills" (Aug 2024): India's AI talent pool will grow from 600–650K to more than 1.25M between 2022 and 2027, with a demand-supply gap as the AI market grows 25–35% a year — [Deloitte press release](https://www.deloitte.com/in/en/about/press-room/bridging-the-ai-talent-gap-to-boost-indias-tech-and-economic-impact-deloitte-nasscom-report.html); [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/india-s-ai-talent-pool-to-grow-to-1-25-million-by-2027-nasscom-deloitte-india-report). Business Standard describes the 1.25M as demand instead — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/industry/news/demand-for-indian-ai-talent-to-double-by-2027-but-quality-a-hurdle-report-124082000896_1.html)
- [PROJECTION] Bain: India's AI job openings could exceed 2.3M by 2027, against a talent pool of ~1.2M — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/indias-ai-sector-poised-to-surpass-23-million-job-openings-by-2027-1.500056470)
- [FACT] TeamLease Digital (Sep 2026): India's talent gap in GenAI and cloud skills is 53–60% — [YourStory, Sep 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/talent-gap-genai-cloud-india-stands-53-60-teamlease-digital-report)
- [FACT] Indian IT industry AI-related revenue was $10–12B in FY26 — [NASSCOM SR 2026 PDF](https://d3r3sr3o54kk15.cloudfront.net/s3fs-public/media_pdf/India%E2%80%99s%20tech%20industry%20continues%20to%20redefine%20AI-led%20transformation,%20efficiency%20and%20skilling--%20Nasscom%20Annual%20Strategic%20Review%202026.pdf)
- [FACT] Indeed India's MD reported in 2026 that 71% of white-collar employees use AI — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/ai-based-jobs-rise-across-sectors-71-of-white-collar-employees-using-ai-indeed-india-md)
- [FACT] World Bank (Oct 2026 update, as reported): AI is not yet causing a straightforward fall in India's overall employment, but it is changing how firms recruit — cited in [StartupWire](https://www.startupwire.in/post/ai-creates-jobs-freshers-entry-level-hiring-market-disruption-india) (secondary)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] The shift from 27% to 40% of large organisations scaling agents in one year is the strongest demand signal for production agent engineers (evals, observability, integration, security). Indian GCCs and IT services firms are well placed to deliver this work for global clients.
- [SPECULATION] Talent-gap figures vary by definition. The consistent signal is a shortage of senior, production-grade AI talent, not of people with AI certificates. NASSCOM counts 2M+ people "upskilled in AI", which suggests supply at the basic level is plentiful.

### Gaps
- The NASSCOM AI Adoption Index 2026 and EY/BCG India-specific agentic AI surveys were not retrieved.
- WEF Future of Jobs 2025 was not re-verified in this session. From training knowledge, it lists AI and big data as the fastest-growing skill — [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/); please verify.

## Q4. FDE demand: global and India

### Takeaway
FDE is the fastest-growing title in AI hiring. Postings grew 800% globally (Jan–Sep 2025) and more than 1,000% YoY (Jan–Aug 2026, Lightcast), and India postings rose about 19-fold YoY at the June 2026 peak. Absolute India volumes are still small (~100–570 listings depending on the source) and skew senior (median ~5 years' experience). OpenAI and Anthropic's India hiring is mainly applied-AI and deployment roles.

### Cited Findings
- [FACT] a16z, 4 Jun 2025: "Trading Margin for Moat: Why the Forward Deployed Engineer Is the Hottest Job in Startups" (analysis, not posting data) — [a16z](https://a16z.com/forward-deployed-job-titles/)
- [FACT] Monthly FDE listings rose more than 800% between Jan and Sep 2025 (Financial Times, also attributed to Indeed) — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/forward-deployed-engineers-emerge-as-one-of-ais-fastest-growing-jobs/); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-anthropic-cohere-ai-hiring/)
- [FACT] Lightcast: FDE postings rose more than 1,000% in Jan–Aug 2026 vs the same period of 2025 — [Fortune, 3 Sep 2026](https://www.fortune.com/2026/09/03/forward-deployed-engineers-fast-growing-six-figure-silicon-valley-job-integrate-ai-with-customers-tech-careers-palantir/). Indeed: +729% YoY in April 2026 — [LeadDev](https://leaddev.com/career-development/the-rise-of-the-forward-deployed-engineer-fde)
- [FACT] India (Indeed): FDE postings rose ~19x YoY in June 2026, ~16x in July and ~6x in August, so growth is decelerating from the peak — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/workforce-planning/demand-for-forward-deployed-engineers-jumps-19-fold-in-india-report-52770)
- [FACT] India snapshot counts: Glassdoor showed ~100–570 FDE-related India listings in Oct 2026 depending on filter — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Job/india-forward-deployed-engineer-jobs-SRCH_IL.0,5_IN115_KO6,31.htm). A tracker counted 138 open FDE and related roles at 84 companies (12 Oct 2026), of which 6 were entry-level, with a median minimum of ~5 years' experience. Bengaluru dominates. Hirers include Cursor, DevRev, Databricks, Notion, Accenture, NTT DATA and ServiceNow — [Masai School](https://www.masaischool.com/blog/forward-deployed-engineer-jobs-in-india/); [Neurapath, Nov 2026: 71 open roles](https://neurapath.in/forward-deployed-engineer/jobs)
- [FACT] Anthropic announced its first India office in Bengaluru (8 Oct 2025, for early 2026). It is Anthropic's second Asian office after Tokyo — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/08/anthropic-to-open-first-india-office-in-2026.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/07/anthropic-plans-to-open-india-office-eyes-tie-up-with-billionaire-ambani). A 16 Feb 2026 opening date appears only in an aggregator and is unverified.
- [FACT] OpenAI has a presence in New Delhi. At the India AI Impact Summit (Feb 2026) it announced Mumbai and Bengaluru offices for later in 2026 — [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/karnataka/after-anthropic-open-ai-to-open-office-in-bengaluru/)
- [FACT] Late 2026, public job boards showed ~7 OpenAI and ~6 Anthropic India openings, mostly Applied AI Architect/Engineer (pre-sales and deployment) — [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news-updates/openai-anthropic-announce-multiple-job-openings-in-india); [DEV roundup](https://dev.to/shamuddin/ai-jobs-this-week-5128-openings-160-in-india-19dg) (secondary)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] FDE is a real but narrow market in India: hundreds of open roles, not tens of thousands. Growth rates are inflated by a near-zero 2024 base, and India YoY multiples fell from 19x to 6x between June and August 2026. Expect the title to normalise (absorbed into "Applied AI Engineer" and "Solutions/Deployment Engineer"), while the underlying skill set (customer-facing, ships production agents, integrates with enterprise systems) stays in demand.
- [SPECULATION] For Indians, the highest-value FDE roles are likely APAC- or global-facing roles at AI labs and AI-native SaaS, plus GCC/IT-services "AI deployment" pods for US clients.

## Q6. Startup funding trends 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Indian tech funding is stable in dollars ($10.3B Jan–Sep 2026, +7% YoY) but deal count fell 38%, so capital is concentrated in fewer, larger rounds. AI funding nearly doubled to ~$1.6B (Tracxn), but 75% of it went to infrastructure while application-layer AI funding fell.

## Q10. Forecast scenarios 2026–2028 (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Expected case: overall Indian tech headcount grows ~2–4% a year, AI/ML postings grow ~15–25% a year, GCCs reach ~2,400–2,600 by 2028, and FDE/applied-AI demand stays strong but normalises. Generic SDE and fresher demand stays flat or weak. **All scenario numbers below are my speculation**, anchored on the dated facts above.

### Cited Findings (anchors used)
- Headcount growth of +2.3% / ~135K net in FY26 — [NASSCOM SR 2026](https://d3r3sr3o54kk15.cloudfront.net/s3fs-public/media_pdf/India%E2%80%99s%20tech%20industry%20continues%20to%20redefine%20AI-led%20transformation,%20efficiency%20and%20skilling--%20Nasscom%20Annual%20Strategic%20Review%202026.pdf)

- 40% of large firms scaling agents (2026) vs 27% the year before — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)
- India FDE postings slowing from 19x to 6x YoY (Jun to Aug 2026) — [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/workforce-planning/demand-for-forward-deployed-engineers-jumps-19-fold-in-india-report-52770)
- Tech funding +7% but deal count −38% (Jan–Sep 2026) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/indias-tech-cos-raise-usd-103b-in-2026-tracxn-report)

### Inferences (all SPECULATION)
**Conservative (probability ~25%).** Triggers: US recession or tariff shock, an AI capex pullback and "ROI disappointment", GPU and power delays in India, and IT services clients cutting discretionary spend.
- Overall tech headcount 0–1% a year. More IT services layoffs at middle management. Fresher intake falls below 2026 levels.
- AI/ML postings growth slows to ~5–10% a year and concentrates in GCCs and Big Tech. Application-AI startups consolidate, and voice AI and wrapper startups fail.
- GCCs grow slowly (~2,250–2,350 by 2028), but cost pressure still pushes work to India, so GCCs are relatively resilient.
- FDE titles shrink at startups. FDE-type work persists inside Big Tech and IT services AI practices.
- Generic SDE, junior, and prompt/RAG roles are hit hardest. Senior engineers with production AI experience stay employable at flat pay.

**Expected (probability ~55%).**
- Overall tech headcount +2–4% a year. NASSCOM-style revenue growth of 5–7%, with AI revenue rising from $10–12B to roughly $20–25B by FY28 (speculative extrapolation).
- AI/ML postings +15–25% a year. AI-skill requirements spread into most SDE job descriptions, so the boundary between "AI engineer" and "software engineer" blurs by 2028.
- GCCs ~2,400–2,600 by 2028, and India's AI talent pool passes ~1.25M (Deloitte-NASSCOM projection) while the senior gap persists.
- FDE and applied-AI roles grow from hundreds to low thousands of openings in India. The title partly merges into "Applied AI Engineer" and "AI Solutions Engineer". Labs (OpenAI, Anthropic) and AI-native SaaS expand India go-to-market teams.
- Junior SDE hiring stays flat in volume, with higher bar expectations (AI-tool fluency, demonstrable projects). IT services keep ~20K/year fresher intake at the largest firms but with lower bench sizes.
- Premium skills: agents, evals, inference, AI security, and domain-plus-AI. Commoditised: generic prompt work, basic RAG, dashboards.

**Optimistic (probability ~20%).** Triggers: enterprise agent adoption broadens to mid-market, H-1B and offshoring pressure accelerates GCC setups, IndiaAI and hyperscaler gigawatt campuses come online on schedule (Vizag Phase 1 around 2027–28), and AI app-layer funding recovers.
- Overall tech headcount +5–6% a year, with a renewed net hiring wave.
- AI/ML postings +30%+ a year, sustained. GCCs pass ~2,700 by 2028 and multiple AI-native GCCs emerge.
- FDE and deployment roles become a mainstream career track, with thousands of India openings, including India-based roles serving US and EU clients remotely at global-band pay.
- Infrastructure buildout creates a distinct MLOps and inference job market. Indic and voice AI produce one or two scale winners hiring hundreds each.
- Even here, routine junior coding demand does not return to 2021–22 levels. The recovery is in AI-augmented and higher-skill roles.

**Robust across all scenarios (higher confidence):** (1) senior and production AI skills earn a premium; (2) generic junior coding and "prompt engineer" titles are oversupplied; (3) GCCs and Big Tech India are a more stable demand base than startups; (4) H-1B costs favour India-based roles over US relocation.

### Gaps
- No institution has published an India tech hiring forecast for 2027–2028, so the scenarios are my construction.
- The NASSCOM 2030 roadmap ($500B+ tech revenue ambition) was not verified in this session.
- Scenario probabilities are subjective.
