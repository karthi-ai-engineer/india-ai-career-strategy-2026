## Q1. Category C and D company tables (domain, HQ, India locations, stage/funding, roles, stack, comp, hiring evidence, why target, careers URL)

### Takeaway
Funding for Indian AI-first startups grew sharply in 2026. Inc42 counts $676 Mn in H1 2026 and about $1.1 Bn over 9M 2026. The money is concentrated in a few names: Sarvam, Emergent and Neysa. Among Indian-HQ product companies, the strongest verified top-of-market comp signals are Rippling India, Atlassian India, Postman, Zerodha and Glean India (levels.fyi, Nov 2026). The large consumer unicorns (CRED, PhonePe, Dream11, Razorpay) pay well at SDE-2. Their data comes mostly from older AmbitionBox pages.

## Q2. Which Indian AI startups raised significant funding in 2025–2026 and are hiring? IndiaAI Mission beneficiaries?

### Takeaway
2026 is a breakout year for Indian AI funding, but it is very concentrated. The largest rounds went to Sarvam, Emergent and Neysa. The IndiaAI Mission has backed about 20 indigenous model proposals: Sarvam, Soket, Gan, Gnani, then eight more including Fractal, Tech Mahindra and BharatGen.

### Cited Findings
