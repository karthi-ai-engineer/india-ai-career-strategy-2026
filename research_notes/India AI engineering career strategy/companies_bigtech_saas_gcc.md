# Big Tech, Product MNCs, Enterprise SaaS/Infra and GCCs in India: hiring targets for a ~2-YOE AI Engineer / FDE (join 2027-2028)

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. About 20 search/fetch calls were used, so coverage is uneven. The large investment announcements, GCC macro data, levels.fyi pay for 4 companies and AI-lab office openings are verified with sources. Per-company tech stacks, India headcounts and most Category B companies were NOT verified in this session. Those are listed as gaps and are not filled from memory.

Labelling used throughout:
- **[V]** = verified, with a source and date.
- **[BG]** = general background knowledge (HQ, domain, careers URL patterns). Not fetched or cited in this session. The report writer should treat [BG] as low-risk but uncited.
- **[INF]** = my inference.

## Q1. Company landscape: domain, HQ, India locations, India size, roles, stack, compensation, hiring evidence, why target, careers URL

### Takeaway
The best-documented India growth signals for 2025-2026 are multi-billion-dollar AI and cloud commitments:
- Google: $15B, Vizag AI hub (2026-2030).
- Microsoft: $17.5B (2026-2029), on top of $3B from Jan 2025.
- Amazon: $35B (Dec 2025) plus another $13B (Jun 2026).
- Databricks: $250M, with 100+ R&D engineers planned for Bengaluru.
- JPMorgan: about 1,000 tech hires.
- NVIDIA: the largest single-tenant office lease in India, in Bengaluru.

On levels.fyi, mid-level (SDE-2-equivalent) median total compensation in India clusters at **about ₹63-70 lakh** at Google, Amazon and Atlassian. Microsoft level 62 is about ₹65 lakh.

## Q3. GCC trends: count, 2025-26 growth, AI/GenAI CoEs, pay

### Takeaway
Nasscom-Zinnov counts **2,117 GCCs / 3,728 units in India (FY26, as of Mar 2026)**, with about **$98.4B revenue** and about **2.36M employees**. More than **1,200 GCCs embed AI/ML** and there are **250+ dedicated AI CoEs**. About 65% of new GCC roles created in 2026 need AI skills. GenAI engineering is the top-paid GCC skill, with a 30-40%+ premium.

### Inferences
- [INF] Average GCC pay (₹18.4L median) is far below Big Tech product companies (₹63-70L at SDE-2-equivalent on levels.fyi). "GCC" is not one pay tier. Tech-first GCCs (Google, Microsoft, Atlassian, Stripe, Broadcom, Databricks) pay at Big Tech levels. Bank and retail GCCs sit in between, and the GenAI premium (30-40%) is the lever there.
- [INF] The scarcity of senior AI talent (e.g., only 320 retail-GCC professionals with 8+ yrs of AI experience) suggests strong demand for 3-5 YOE applied GenAI engineers who can own production systems. This is the profile for a 2027-28 move.
